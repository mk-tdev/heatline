# Heatline — judges’ pitch

About 75 seconds, excluding the live demo. Rehearse aloud and leave space for the opening and closing lines.

## Script

It’s three in the afternoon. The midday break is over—but the heat isn’t.

A concrete truck is coming. The deck is exposed. And Anwar is only on his second day.

Heatline turns that moment into a decision you can explore.

Inside this live 3D construction site, historical weather drives twenty synthetic workers. Guardian explains each worker’s guidance in their language—and speaks it aloud. Noor shows supervisors what their crews need. Omar checks conditions, tests plans, and shows his work.

Rules set the warnings and check the plans. The supervisor decides.

Then we replay the same afternoon with one decision changed—and compare the consequences.

This is a working prototype, not a clinically validated prediction system. We show missed warnings and remaining exposure, because credible safety tools must make their limits visible.

We’re looking for a construction partner and an occupational-health expert to help validate the next step.

Heatline. Different stories. The same right to go home well.
