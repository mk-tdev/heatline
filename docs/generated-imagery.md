## Design and motion

The hero uses a slow 32-second alternating camera drift and soft four-second site-marker pulses. The pause control disables ambient animation. Reduced-motion preferences disable these effects and smooth scrolling. Scenario panels are frosted translucent layers over the full 3D canvas on desktop; mobile uses stacked panels with a dedicated scene viewport. Important warnings and plan checks retain stronger surface opacity. Reduced-transparency and unsupported backdrop-filter fallbacks use near-opaque surfaces.
